## 5. Cost Optimization Strategies

| Strategy | Savings | Implementation |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| **Reserved Instances** (AWS) | 30-40% on ECS/RDS | 1-year commitment at Scale phase |
| **Spot Instances** (AI/GPU) | 60-70% on GPU | Use spot for non-critical AI batch jobs |
| **CDN Caching** | 50-70% on bandwidth | Aggressive edge caching for images/static |
| **DB Connection Pooling** | 80% fewer connections | pgBouncer at Growth phase |
| **Image Compression** | 60-80% on storage | WebP/AVIF with Cloudflare Images |
| **Auto-scaling** | 40% on compute | Scale to zero during off-peak hours |
| **Open Source** | 100% on license fees | Use self-hosted MeiliSearch vs Algolia |
